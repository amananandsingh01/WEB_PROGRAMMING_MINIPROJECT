## 🚀 How to Use

### Dashboard:
1. Navigate to Dashboard.html
2. Scroll through to see:
   - Your achievement badges at the top
   - Learning progress milestones
   - Recent activity timeline
   - Your network connections
   - Recommended skills to learn

### Achievements Page:
1. Click "View All" in the Achievement Badges section
2. Browse earned, in-progress, and locked badges
3. Click any badge to see details

### Learning Paths:
1. Click "Learning Paths" in navigation
2. Filter by difficulty or view all
3. Click "Start" to begin a path or "View Path" for details
4. Track your progress on each path

### Community:
1. Click "Community" in navigation
2. Filter by mentor type, rising stars, or rating
3. Click "View Profile" to see details
4. Click "Message" to start learning
